## Full description (max 4,000 chars)
```
H2HFleet is a fleet management platform built for small and mid-sized logistics operators. Live vehicle tracking, expense control, and maintenance planning live in one app — with no expensive GPS hardware required to get started.

■ LIVE VEHICLE TRACKING
• See every vehicle on one map, updated continuously over a realtime connection
• Driver mode turns a crew member's phone into a tracking device — no hardware to buy
• Review route history, speed, and travel time after the fact
• Plan multi-stop routes with distance and estimated time for each leg

■ COST CONTROL
• Log fuel, tolls, repairs, and fines per vehicle and per driver
• Attach receipt photos from the camera or gallery — no paper to file
• See cost per vehicle, per month, and per kilometre
• Export records for your accounting workflow

■ MAINTENANCE AND DOCUMENTS
• Set service intervals by odometer reading or by date
• Get reminders before insurance and annual road tax fall due
• Keep a complete service history for every vehicle

■ TRIPS AND DRIVERS
• Create trips, assign drivers, and follow status through to completion
• Manage your driver roster with role-based access
• Each company's data is isolated at the database level

■ AI ASSISTANT
• Ask plain-language questions such as "which vehicle burned the most fuel this month"
• Get automatic fleet summaries and flags on unusual spending

■ LINE NOTIFICATIONS
• Push trip, expense, and maintenance alerts to your team's LINE account
• Nothing important gets missed, even with the app closed

■ WORKS EVERYWHERE
• Phone, tablet, and web browser share the same data
• Separate views designed for managers and for drivers

■ WHO IT IS FOR
• Small and mid-sized transport and logistics businesses
• Contractors running several pickups or trucks
• Service businesses with field teams
• Vehicle rental and staff shuttle operators

■ PRIVACY
Location access is used to show vehicle positions on the map while the app is in use. Managers must inform drivers and obtain consent before enabling tracking, in line with Thailand's PDPA. We do not sell or trade personal data for marketing.

Try the web version at h2hfleet.netlify.app
Support via LINE Official Account @655jmtme
```
